# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js, FastAPI, SQLite, FAISS and HydraDB as specified in the project brief.

## Users

On-call engineers comparing a new alert with previous incidents. Platform leads evaluating historical retrieval quality.

## Product Purpose

Surface useful past incidents with evidence, and let responders record which precedent helped once an incident is resolved.

## Positioning

Confirmed resolutions create traversable relationships between incidents. A time-ordered backtest evaluates their value.

## Operating Context

The engineer starts with symptoms, compares two sets of evidence, inspects a path and records an outcome. The learning report shows the evaluation protocol and provenance.

## Brand Commitments

Near-black surfaces, warm white type, and orange for primary actions and confirmed relationships. Use self-hosted Bricolage Grotesque for reading and IBM Plex Mono for identifiers. Use Streamline Pixel Mono Free icons beside functional text labels. The homepage uses a scroll-driven constellation to show the difference between proximity and a recorded relationship. The analysis workspace leads with a connected precedent and its evidence path.

## Evidence on Hand

The design brief specifies GitLab as the research corpus. Recorded synthetic examples demonstrate workflows before a live corpus and account are available.

## Product Principles

- Give the strongest evidence a clear place and keep the vector baseline easy to inspect.
- Ground claims in visible sources.
- Identify the provenance of every run.
